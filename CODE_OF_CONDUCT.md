# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

ModelDock aims to be a respectful and constructive open-source project.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.

For serious or sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the repository's security or contact channels.
