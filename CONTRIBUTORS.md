# Contributors

ModelDock is built in public, and every contribution matters.

This page recognizes people who contribute code, documentation, tests, design, bug fixes, reviews, ideas, and other meaningful improvements to the project.

## Contributor Wall

| Contributor | Contribution |
|---|---|

> **Want to be on the wall?**
>
> Pick an open issue, follow the contribution guidelines, and open a focused pull request. Every accepted contribution gets recognized here.

## How recognition works

Contributors are added when their contribution is merged into ModelDock. The entry can briefly describe what they contributed, so the wall reflects the project's history rather than acting as a leaderboard.

Thank you to everyone who takes the time to improve ModelDock. 🚀
