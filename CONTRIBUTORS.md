# Contributors

ModelDock is built in public, and every contribution matters.

This page recognizes community contributors who contribute code, documentation, tests, design, bug fixes, reviews, ideas, and other meaningful improvements to the project.

## Want to be on the wall?

Pick an open issue, follow the contribution guidelines, and open a focused pull request. Every accepted community contribution gets recognized here.

## How recognition works

The project maintainer is intentionally not listed. Community contributors are added when their contribution is merged into ModelDock. The wall records merged PR counts and a short summary of meaningful contributions.

Thank you to everyone who takes the time to improve ModelDock. 🚀
