# Contributors

ModelDock is built in public, and every contribution matters.

This page recognizes community contributors who contribute code, documentation, tests, design, bug fixes, reviews, ideas, and other meaningful improvements.

## Contributor Wall

Community-only recognition. The maintainer is intentionally excluded.

**Point system:** **10**, **25**, or **50** points are awarded based on the scope of the work. Points are awarded after the PR is merged and tracked for contributor recognition.

## Want to be on the wall?

Pick an open issue, follow the contribution guidelines, and open a focused pull request. Every accepted community contribution gets recognized here.

Thank you to everyone who takes the time to improve ModelDock. 🚀
